Pizza

Description

This homemade pizza is easy to make and tastes better than takeout. With a crispy crust, flavorful sauce, and your favorite toppings, it’s perfect for dinner or entertaining guests.

Customize it with vegetables, meats, or extra cheese for the ultimate comfort food experience.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Toppings of your choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, etc.)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let sit for 5–10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix and knead the dough until smooth. Let it rise for 1 hour.
  3. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  4. Roll out the dough, spread sauce, sprinkle cheese, and add toppings.
  5. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  6. Let cool slightly, slice, and enjoy!
