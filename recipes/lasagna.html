Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe features layers of rich meat sauce, tender noodles, and a creamy cheese filling. Perfect for family dinners or meal prep!

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a skillet, cook ground beef until browned. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper.
  4. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella. Repeat layers and finish with Parmesan on top.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake an additional 10–15 minutes until bubbly.
  6. Let it rest 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
