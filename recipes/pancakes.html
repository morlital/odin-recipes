Pancakes

Description

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are perfect for a weekend breakfast. Serve them with syrup, fruit, or butter.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. In a bowl, sift together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in milk, egg, and melted butter. Mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour batter onto the griddle. Cook until bubbles form and edges are dry, then flip and cook the other side.
  5. Serve warm with toppings of your choice.
